The Five Koshas

The pañca-kośha teaching describes five progressively subtler sheaths through which we experience body, energy, mind, discernment, and bliss.

From the physical to the subtle

Move from the outermost sheath inward: body → energy → mind → wisdom → bliss. The PSSM column offers the closest conceptual relationship, not a literal equivalence.

A Careful Comparison

Related frameworks, not identical maps

PSSM correspondences are approximate

The five-kośha teaching and the PSSM bodies come from different frameworks. Their terms should not be mapped as exact one-to-one equivalents. The PSSM body shown for each kośha is the closest conceptual relationship only; in particular, both Vijñānamaya and Ānandamaya can overlap with aspects described as causal.

Body→Energy→Mind→Wisdom→Bliss

Beyond the Five Sheaths

Ātman is not another kośha

Even Ānandamaya remains a sheath. In the traditional teaching, Ātman—the pure Self—is beyond all five kośhas. The closest PSSM concept may be the Spiritual Body or Soul, but this too is a conceptual comparison rather than an exact equivalence.
