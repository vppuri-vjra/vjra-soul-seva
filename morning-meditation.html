Today’s reflection · Two trishuls of light and wishes of kindness
01 · Wisdom cardTwo Trishuls of LightThree ways to grow in wisdom, and three ways to meet the world peacefully.+
✦

The trishul of learning

  • ध्यान — meditation
  • स्वाध्याय — study and reflection
  • सज्जन सांगत्य — good company

Its light is ज्ञान — wisdom.

✦

The trishul of daily conduct

  • No judgment
  • No complaint
  • No unnecessary interference

A way to remain peaceful and respectful without trying to control others.

Guided meditation script

“Before we begin, I invite you to imagine two trishuls—two sources of light that can accompany us in everyday life.

The first reminds us of three ways to grow: ध्यान, spending time in meditation; स्वाध्याय, learning and reflecting; and सज्जन सांगत्य, being with people who help us grow. Together, these bring the light of ज्ञान—understanding.

The second reminds us how to meet the world: no judgment, no complaint, and no unnecessary interference. We can notice what is happening without immediately labeling it, resisting it, or trying to control it.

We can begin practising that right now. As we sit, a thought may come. We do not need to judge it. A sound may arise. We do not need to complain about it. The breath may feel different from what we expect. We do not need to interfere with it.

Now, sit comfortably and allow your eyes to close, if that feels comfortable. As the music begins, simply notice your natural breath. Feel the air coming in and going out. There is no need to breathe deeply or change its rhythm.

Whenever your attention wanders, that is all right. Gently return to the breath. Again and again, we return—letting these two lights guide our practice.”

02 · Wisdom cardMiraculous ThoughtA kindness practice for ourselves, others, and all beings.+

Miraculous thinking is choosing a kind thought that can open a new way of feeling and relating to others. The “miracle” is the possibility of a gentler perspective, not a promise that we will feel good immediately.

Kind thought → feeling of warmth or ease → kinder next thought

Thoughts and feelings can influence one another. As our emotional state changes, the brain and body also communicate through chemical messengers. Repeating these wishes may help some people feel more positive or connected; no particular feeling or chemical response is guaranteed.

Wishes of kindness

  1. May I be happy.मैं सुखी रहूँ।
  2. May my loved ones be happy.मेरे प्रियजन सुखी रहें।
  3. May people I do not know be happy.अपरिचित लोग सुखी रहें।
  4. May people I find difficult be happy.जिन लोगों के साथ मेरा संबंध कठिन है, वे भी सुखी रहें।
  5. May all beings, everywhere, be happy.हर जगह सभी प्राणी सुखी रहें।
  6. May all beings be peaceful.सभी प्राणी शांत रहें।
  7. May all beings be well.सभी प्राणी कुशल रहें।

These words help us open our hearts, feel calmer, and extend kindness to ourselves and others.

ये शब्द हमारे मन में करुणा जगाते हैं, हमें शांत करते हैं और अपने तथा दूसरों के प्रति दयालु बनाते हैं।
