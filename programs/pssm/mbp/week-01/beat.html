Week 1 · Conscious energy

BEAT

Body · Energy · Amplification · Technology

BEAT brings awareness to the physical, emotional and everyday patterns through which energy is either conserved or dissipated.

Definition

What is BEAT?
“The technology by which we can enhance the body’s energy and maintain it at a peak level, at all times, so that it may enable us to act in the most appropriate manner, always.”

Concept given by Brahmarshi Patriji

Conscious physical and emotional patterns conserve, enhance and sustain energy. Unconscious or purposeless patterns dissipate it.

Patterns

Conscious living, one pattern at a time

01

Eating

Scientific, need-based and conscious food intake increases the physical body’s energy.

02

Water Intake

Drinking pure water frequently, sufficiently and consciously supports physical energy.

03

Speaking

Purposeful, brief and conscious speech conserves energy; clarity matters more than style.

04

Seeing

Purposeful and conscious seeing conserves the energy otherwise spent through the eyes.

05

Sleeping

Conscious sleeping patterns—including posture, split rest and breath awareness—help restore energy.

06

Breathing

Conscious breath-watching allows a greater inflow of cosmic energy along with oxygen.

07

Thinking

Scientific, purposeful and conscious thinking conserves the energies of the physical body.

08

Emotions

Courage, compassion, oneness and frankness enhance energy; negative emotions deplete it.

09

Physical Touch

Friendship- and affection-based touch tends to energize the physical body.

10

Routine Work

Performing routine activities consciously and as beautiful arts enhances physical energy.

11

Worldly Vocations

Choose work suited to your aptitudes, physical needs and true spiritual purpose.
