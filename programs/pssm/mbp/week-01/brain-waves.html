Brain Waves

A study table based on the language and key ideas in our reading module.

The table preserves the words and associations used in our module notes, moving from the fully alert conscious mind into relaxation, meditation and deeper states.

Brain-wave bands at a glance
Brain waveFrequencyState of mindReading-module notes
Gammaγ · fastest30–100 HzSuper-conscious mind.
High happiness, compassion and intelligence.		Intense focus / engaged. Universal intelligence—feeling deeply connected to everyone and everything. High happiness, creativity, compassion and intelligence. The brain is supercharged and processes information quickly. Seen in high achievers and highly spiritual people.
Betaβ · active13–30 HzConscious mind.
Fully alert / awake—outer world.		Stress, bipolar disorder, anger, fear and anxiety. Insomnia and excessive beta.
Alphaα · relaxed7–13 HzFirst-layer subconscious mind.Meditation / relaxation begins. Effortless creativity. Learn, process, remember and recall large amounts of information fast and with peak efficiency. Highly creative people have bursts of alpha waves when they have a great idea. Fear and phobias decrease; comfort, peace, happiness and harmony increase.
Thetaθ · inward4–7 HzDeep subconscious / unconscious mind.
Expansive; upon waking.		Go deeper into meditation / relaxation. Flashes of creative visualization from vivid imagery. More open and connected to other people. Insight / intuition. Impressed thoughts and increased motivation. Full ecstasy.
Deltaδ · slowest0–4 HzUnconscious mind.No thinking. Deep sleep and body regeneration; entry into the non-physical state / realm.
Reading-module language: The descriptions above reproduce the concepts and terminology from our notes. In EEG science, brain-wave bands overlap and do not by themselves prove a specific emotion, spiritual state or medical condition.
