Week 1 · Understanding meditation

Brain Waves

The brain is always active. What changes is the pattern of its electrical rhythms—and meditation can gently shift which patterns become more prominent.

Brain waves are rhythmic patterns measured with an EEG. The five bands below often overlap; no single band represents one fixed emotion, ability or level of consciousness.

Gamma30–100 Hz
Beta13–30 Hz
Alpha7–13 Hz
Theta4–7 Hz
Delta0.5–4 Hz
Brain-wave bands at a glance
Brain waveFrequencyCommonly associated withIn meditation
Gammaγ · fastest30–100 HzIntense focus, integrating information, learning and complex mental processing.Some experienced meditators show increased gamma activity during compassion or open-awareness practices, but this varies by person and technique.
Betaβ · active13–30 HzNormal waking alertness, active thinking, problem-solving and attention. Very high arousal can accompany stress or anxiety.Often prominent before practice, when attention is directed outward or the mind is busy. It does not mean that something is wrong.
Alphaα · relaxed7–13 HzRelaxed wakefulness, especially with eyes closed; calm attention and reduced processing of outside stimuli.Frequently increases during relaxed, effortless meditation and is often used as a marker of settling inward.
Thetaθ · inward4–7 HzDrowsiness, early sleep, memory processes and internally focused imagery.May become more prominent in deeper relaxation, visualization or sustained internal attention. Experiences can include vivid imagery or intuitive insights.
Deltaδ · slowest0.5–4 HzDeep, dreamless non-REM sleep and reduced awareness of the outside world.Delta is primarily a deep-sleep rhythm. Slow-wave activity can appear during some advanced practices, but it should not be treated as a required meditation goal.
How to use this table: Think of these bands as overlapping ingredients, not separate levels on a ladder. An EEG records a mixture of rhythms at the same time, and the meaning depends on where the activity occurs, what the person is doing and how the measurement was made.
