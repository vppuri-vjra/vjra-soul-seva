Brahmarshi Patriji · A model of inner alignment

The Five Finger Concept

A simple way to understand the visible and invisible dimensions of human existence—and their underlying unity.

Patriji uses the five fingers as a simple model for understanding human existence and spirituality.

The main idea is that a person consists of several interconnected dimensions: the body, mind, intellect, individual soul, and Supreme Soul. The body is visible, while the others are invisible. The hand represents their underlying unity—even though the fingers look separate, they are all part of one hand.

BodyThe visible dimension
MindDesires and emotions
IntellectGuidance and discernment
Individual SoulThe peaceful witness
Supreme SoulUnity and higher consciousness

The mind produces desires and is naturally restless. The intellect should guide and discipline the mind. Its highest purpose, however, is not merely practical thinking—it is to help us recognize the soul. The soul itself remains a peaceful witness.

The thumb’s apparent separation from the other fingers symbolizes the Supreme Soul. When the finger representing the individual soul or awakened intellect joins the thumb, it represents yoga: the union of the individual soul with the Supreme Soul through meditation.

Patriji’s broader message

  • Identifying only with the body keeps human life at an instinctive or animal level.
  • Recognizing the mind makes us more fully human.
  • Awakening and properly using the intellect leads toward wisdom.
  • Looking inward through meditation reveals the soul.
  • Realizing the unity of the individual soul and Supreme Soul leads to liberation from suffering and the cycle of birth and death.

In essence: The five-finger concept teaches that our seemingly separate dimensions—and seemingly separate individuals—ultimately belong to one spiritual reality. Meditation is the means of experiencing that unity.
