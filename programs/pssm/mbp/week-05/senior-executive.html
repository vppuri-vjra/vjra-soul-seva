Senior Executive · Leadership Application

The Five Finger Concept

Five connected dimensions that bring leadership capability, stakeholder commitment, judgment, values and purpose into alignment.

Like five fingers forming one hand, the dimensions of executive leadership become effective when they support one another. The example below applies the framework to leading a digital transformation.

3-Minute Briefing for Senior Executives

Audience: Executive leadership team

Application: Developing and scaling a new product

Briefing script

Good morning, everyone.

I would like to share a leadership idea using something we all have—our hand.

Please look at your five fingers. Each finger is different, but the hand becomes effective only when all five work together. Leadership is similar.

The five fingers represent Body, Mind, Intellect, Core Values and Organizational Purpose.

Let us connect these dimensions with brain waves and the example of developing a new product.

Brain waves are patterns of electrical activity in the brain. Their speed changes when we sleep, relax, imagine, concentrate or process information. We will use five brain-wave ranges as a leadership analogy.

Five-Finger dimensionBrain waveBenefitOrganizational application
BodyDeltaDeep rest, recovery and repairProtects employee work–life balance and supports sustainable performance.
MindThetaImagination, creativity and insightHelps teams understand customer problems and generate new product ideas.
IntellectBetaConcentration, analysis and problem-solvingHelps leaders evaluate cost, risk, technology, feasibility and scalability.
Core ValuesAlphaCalm and receptive awarenessGuides leaders to consider whether the product is safe, ethical and beneficial to customers and employees.
Organizational PurposeGammaIntegration and broader understandingConnects the product with customer value, business strategy and long-term direction.

These connections are a teaching analogy. The five dimensions are not controlled by five separate brain waves.

Ānāpānasati meditation helps align the Body, Mind and Intellect. We breathe naturally and observe the incoming and outgoing breath. This settles the body, calms the mind and supports clearer thinking.

The leadership flow is:

Purpose defines why. The Mind imagines what. Core Values guide what is right. The Intellect determines how. The Body delivers sustainably.

When all five dimensions work together, employees find meaning, customers receive responsible value, stakeholders develop trust and the organization grows sustainably.

Thank you.
