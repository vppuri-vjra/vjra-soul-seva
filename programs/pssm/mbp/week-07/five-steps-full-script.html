Manifestation
Five Steps of Creation.

A cleaned, facilitator-ready rendering of Shreans Daga’s guided meditation, organized by its original sequence and timing.

Five steps: Brain coherence · Heart coherence · Creative visualization · Surrender · Gratitude

00Introduction · 0:20–8:31

Welcome and the Five-Step Map

“Greetings, everyone. This is Shreans Daga. Thank you for your time, and welcome to this meditation on manifestation—the five steps of creation.”

“Take a comfortable position. Sit upright, clasp your hands, cross your legs and gently close your eyes. Think of one intention or potential you want to bring into your life.”

“This teaching proposes that potentials exist in the present moment and that our task is to align thought with an elevated emotion. Thoughts are described as the language of the brain and feelings as the language of the body. In this framework, clear intention and elevated emotion form the inner signature of a new possibility.”

“If the intention is abundance, perhaps its emotional reward is freedom. Practise the feeling of freedom before the outer result appears. Become a host to your future rather than a hostage to your past.”

“First, brain coherence: quiet the mind, release resistance and loosen identification with the familiar self. Second, heart coherence: open the heart, connect with your inner being and cultivate an elevated emotional state. Third, creative visualization: impress the goal upon the subconscious mind and mentally rehearse it as present. Imagine what you want, while remaining open about how it arrives. Fourth, surrender: offer the creation to a greater intelligence and allow room for surprise. Fifth, gratitude: give thanks for what you have created in consciousness; gratitude is treated here as the state of receivership.”

01Brain coherence · 8:40–24:48

Become Present with the Breath

“Gently shift awareness to the nostrils. Take one deep breath in through the nose. As you breathe out, relax. Take one more slow, steady breath in. As you exhale, let the whole body relax.”

“Shift awareness from the world around you to the world within you. At this moment, nothing else exists but the breath. Be with the breath—normal, natural, simple, easy and relaxed.”

“For now, set down your name, roles, personality, ego and analytical mind. Withdraw attention from people, places, things, time and events. Rest with the in-breath and the out-breath.”

“Observe every breath entering and leaving the nostrils. Notice its temperature. Is it warmer or cooler? Is the breath deep or shallow? Do not try to correct it. Observe it. Let no breath pass without awareness.”

“Become nobody, no one, nothing, nowhere—in no time. Become pure consciousness, aware that you are aware in a vast field. Surrender the limited self to the greater self. Lose yourself in spaciousness.”

Remain in silent breath awareness until approximately 24:48. Offer brief reminders every few minutes: “Natural breath.” “Notice.” “Return.” “Aware that you are aware.”

02Heart coherence · 24:48–38:19

Open the Heart and Elevate Emotion

“Gently shift awareness to the area of the heart. Allow the breath to move in and out through this center—more deeply, slowly and completely.”

“As you breathe from the heart, focus on feeling. With every breath, bring more life to this center. Where attention goes, energy flows. With every breath, open and awaken the heart.”

“Feel expansion in this area. Feel unlimited. To create an unlimited experience, practise the feeling of being unlimited. To express magnificence, feel magnificent. Tune in to the positive energy of your inner being. Know that your source is your ultimate resource.”

“Breathe, feel and move into a new state of being. Feel joy, gratitude and love. Be thankful for what you have. Feel satisfaction, contentment and fulfilment. Release resistance and move into acceptance and allowance.”

“Radiate this feeling beyond the body. Remember the feeling in your heart. Embrace it. Fall in love with your dream. Let the body memorize this elevated emotion.”

Continue with spacious silence until approximately 38:19, occasionally repeating: “Breathe through the heart.” “Feel gratitude.” “Remember this feeling.”

03Creative visualization · 38:19–48:34

See the Intention as Present

“Return to the intention you chose. In the language of this meditation, tune into that potential. Move into a new state of being.”

“Imagine it clearly, as if it is happening now. What do you see? Who is present? What has changed? How would you feel if it had already happened?”

“Join the clear intention with an elevated emotion. Open the heart and allow the body to respond to a new mind and new feeling. Let the thought become an inner experience, and let the experience create emotion.”

“Teach the body emotionally what that future would feel like now. Feel it with all the senses. From this state of being, imagine living that life. What choices would you make? What behavior would you demonstrate? How would you walk, speak and respond?”

“Invest attention in the future and in possibility. Embrace it. Feel it. Embody it. Notice it. Experience it. Enjoy it. See the dream alive in the mind and heart. When things change within, the way you engage with life changes around you.”

Allow silence for the participant’s own scene. Remind them gently: “See it.” “Feel it.” “Include the next constructive action.”

04Surrender · 48:34–52:10

Release the Route

“Now surrender the intention to a greater mind. Release the creation to infinite intelligence, and allow it to be organized in a way that is right for you and aligned with your highest good.”

“You do not need to control every step or predict every route. Keep the intention clear. Remain available to guidance, learning, opportunity and surprise.”

“Surrender. Trust.”

Rest in silence. Let the image dissolve while retaining the feeling and willingness to act.

05Gratitude and close · 52:10–58:40

Receive with Gratitude

“Place both hands over the heart. Acknowledge this sacred creative center and the power within you. Rest awareness here. Breathe through the heart and allow the breath to relax the body.”

“Give thanks to your new life. Open the heart and offer immense gratitude for what you have created in this meditation. In this teaching, gratitude is the emotional signature of receiving.”

“Breathe and open your focus. Receive the energy of gratitude as it moves through your being. Be thankful for life, for possibility and for the resources already present. Let the feeling become steady.”

“See. Breathe. Smile. Remember this feeling in your heart. Memorize it. Anchor it. Treasure it. How would you walk if the intention were alive in you? Be inspired. Be lifted. Radiate love into life.”

“Now bring awareness back to the physical body. Notice the breath, the hands and the feet. When you are ready, gently open your eyes.”

“Carry the feeling forward—and give it form through your next conscious action.”

Facilitator Integrity

  • Invite participants to choose intentions within their sphere of influence.
  • Never imply that illness, hardship or delayed outcomes reflect inadequate belief.
  • Present quantum-field language as the source’s spiritual framework, not verified causal science.
  • Close with an observable next action so visualization supports—not replaces—responsible behavior.
